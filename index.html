    Current role

    Scientist and PhD researcher

    I work at the Department for Integrated Sensor Systems at the University for Continuing Education Krems while pursuing a doctorate in Physics at the University of Vienna. My research is part of the Christian Doppler Laboratory for magnet design through physics-informed machine learning.

    What guides my work

    Useful models need reliable uncertainty.

    Materials data can be limited, heterogeneous, and costly to obtain. I focus on methods that report what a model knows as well as what it does not know—so predictions can support better optimization decisions.

    Research focus

    From material data to decisions that can be trusted.

    My work combines computational materials design, surrogate modeling, and optimization to support sustainable permanent-magnet development.
