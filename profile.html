About

I am pursuing a doctorate in Physics at the University of Vienna while working as a Scientist at the Department for Integrated Sensor Systems, University for Continuing Education Krems. My work is part of the Christian Doppler Laboratory for magnet design through physics-informed machine learning.

My background spans Biology and Computational Science. It informs a cross-disciplinary approach to complex natural-science questions, combining data analysis, modeling, simulation, and software development.
